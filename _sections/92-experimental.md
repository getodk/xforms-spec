---
title: Experimental features
---

Developers may introduce experimental features not yet ready for documentation or production use. In that case, they are encouraged to use the `x-` prefix to indicate that the functionality is experimental and not intended for ongoing use. Features with an `x-` prefix may be changed or removed without warning. Once a feature is no longer experimental, it should get a new identifier(s) without the `x-` prefix before being added to this specification.
